Roadmap

What's here, what's next, and what we're exploring.

Every capability on this page carries one of four labels, so it's always clear what you get by installing Nodeau today and what we're still building.

Available

All of this is in the current release (), on a Linux machine with an NVIDIA GPU or an Apple Silicon Mac. It's what you get by following the install guide.

Getting started

One-command installer

Available

Downloads Nodeau, checks its checksum and puts it in a folder you own.

Setup that shows its plan

Available

nodeau install checks the machine, prints every privileged command, and adopts the pieces that are already there.

nodeau doctor

Available

A read-only check of the machine and the installation, with a remedy for every finding.

nodeau quickstart

Available

A verified model, a fit check, a running workload and a local endpoint in one command.

Upgrading in place

Available

Run the installer again. nodeau update tells you when something newer is out, and can download and verify it for you.

Clean uninstall

Available

Prints its plan first, and keeps your driver, your models and everything Nodeau found already installed.

Apple Silicon Macs

Available

Chat models on the Mac's own GPU through Metal, with no password needed to install. A Mac runs standalone.

Models and tasks

A catalog with a hardware ladder

Available

Eight curated models, from 8 GB cards up to 24 GB or two cards in one machine. Each one says which card size it's for.

Verified downloads

Available

Pinned to an exact file from the publisher and checked against a known SHA-256 before anything runs it.

Fit checks before anything starts

Available

A measurement where Nodeau has one, a careful estimate otherwise, and the decision says which.

Accepting a fit risk yourself

Available

--accept-estimate-risk, for when the only thing in the way is Nodeau's extra margin on unmeasured hardware.

OpenAI-compatible API

Available

Chat and completions with streaming, on 127.0.0.1 with a key made on your machine.

Embeddings and reranking

Available

Vectors for search and RAG, and a rerank route in the Jina and Cohere shape. On Linux with NVIDIA.

Structured output and tool calling

Available

JSON that follows your schema, and tool calls with arguments your code can parse. On Linux with NVIDIA.

Vision

Available

Send an image with your prompt and ask about it. On Linux with NVIDIA.

Bring your own model

Available

Import a GGUF file, and qualification proves on a real card what it can do. Your weights stay on your machines. On Linux with NVIDIA.

Batch inference

Available

A file of chat or embedding requests, results matched to every input, and several workers at once, each on its own card. Home Pro and Business, on Linux.

Hardware and scheduling

Every GPU scheduled on its own

Available

Each card in a machine is its own resource and runs one workload at a time. Home Pro puts up to two cards in a machine to work.

One model across several GPUs

Available

For a model too big for any one card, Nodeau splits it across cards in the same Linux machine, and each card holds its own share. It adds capacity rather than speed: on our test pair, a model that fit on one card ran about 21% slower split across two.

Scheduling you can steer

Available

Efficiency, Balanced or Performance, for the whole fleet or one machine. Balanced is the default.

Placement you can read

Available

nodeau placement explain shows why a workload is where it is, and what the alternatives would have cost.

Power limits and budgets

Available

Cap what a card may draw, inside the card's own range, and set a power budget placement has to respect.

Workloads that stay put

Available

A running workload stays exactly where Nodeau placed it, even when another card scores better. If its machine stops reporting, the work is held in place until the machine is back.

Fleet

Two commands to add a machine

Available

nodeau fleet invite on the machine you have, nodeau join on the new one. Home Pro runs up to three machines.

Your fleet in a browser

Available

Every machine, card and workload at app.nodeau.ai, on every plan. Your machines connect out to it.

Remote operation

Available

Start and stop models, set scheduling, drain machines, read logs and recover a workload from a machine that's gone. Home Pro and Business.

Machine health and alerts

Available

Processor, memory, storage, network and GPU for every machine, a day of history, and alerts when something needs you. The readings stay on your hardware.

Drain for maintenance

Available

New work goes elsewhere and everything already running keeps serving until you're done.

Each machine checks its own models

Available

Every machine hashes its own copy of a model before it's offered work that needs it.

Organisation

Your own limits

Available

Quotas on workloads, GPUs and batch workers, plus which models, cards and machines a fleet may use. Lowering a limit leaves running work alone.

Usage in accelerator-hours

Available

From the scheduler's own record of what was reserved, with an hourly rate of your own if you'd like a cost figure.

A record of changes

Available

Who changed what, including requests that were turned down, kept for a year.

Know what every machine runs

Available

nodeau update compares every machine with the published release. A release channel only moves forward.

Entitlements that travel offline

Available

Carry a signed entitlement to a machine with no internet. It's checked exactly like one fetched over the network.

Home Pro, on sale

Available

$9.99 a month, with checkout and self-service plan changes in your account.

Upgrades

Upgrade planning

Available

nodeau fleet upgrade plan pins a release to an exact build and shows what moving each machine would involve, in order and with the work on it, before anything changes.

In progress

Being built or tested now. Each of these arrives in a future release.

Fleet upgrades from one place

In progress

Pick a release, approve the plan you saw, and let Nodeau move the fleet one machine at a time, checking each one before the next.

Maintenance windows and release channels

In progress

Set when Nodeau may act on its own, in your own time zone, and which build your fleet should be on, right from your account. Upgrade plans will follow both.

More on the Mac

In progress

Embeddings, reranking, vision and several models at once on Apple Silicon.

Several people in one organisation

In progress

Members with roles, groups from your identity provider, and keys for scripts that can only ever do less than their owner. For Business.

Single sign-on

In progress

Sign in through your organisation's identity provider. For Business.

Automatic recovery

In progress

You can recover a workload from a machine that's gone from the console today. Having Nodeau bring it back up on another machine by itself, safely, is being built and tested.

More hardware, measured

In progress

More cards measured rather than estimated, more model profiles, and more Linux and macOS versions. Tell us about your hardware!
