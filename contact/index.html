Design partners

Nodeau reasons across mixed GPU machines already. What it hasn’t met is your hardware, and the single command that adds a machine is what we are building now.

If your team has GPUs — workstations, a lab server, a few racks — we would like to test against them and work out with you how fleet operation, policy and packaging ought to behave. A real conversation about your machines, not a waitlist.
