Why there's no per-token price

Your GPU does the work

The compute is hardware you already paid for. We'd rather charge for making it useful than for your own electricity.

The value is running it

Working out what fits, keeping it running and knowing what's where. That grows with machines, so that's what plans follow.

Costs you can plan

Owned hardware has a known capacity and a known cost. For steady work that's a comfortable shape to budget around.

The economics depend on how busy your machines are. Hardware that runs work most of the day gets the most out of owning it.
