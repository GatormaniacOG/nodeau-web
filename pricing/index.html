Pricing

Priced for infrastructure, not for tokens.

Your hardware does the work. Nodeau's job is making that hardware usable and manageable, so nothing here is metered per prompt, per token or per request.

Why there is no per-token price

Your GPU does the work

The work happens on hardware you already paid for. Charging per token would be charging you for your own electricity.

The value is management

Deciding what fits, keeping it running, and knowing what is where. That scales with machines, not with prompts.

Capacity you can predict

Owned hardware has a fixed ceiling and a fixed cost. That is a different shape from per-request billing, and for some workloads a better one.

Owned hardware is not automatically cheaper than cloud inference. It depends entirely on utilisation, and the honest answer for a machine that sits idle is usually no.

Start on the machine you already have.

Home is free, needs no account, and will tell you straight whether your machine can run it.
