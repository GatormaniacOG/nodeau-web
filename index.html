THE IDEA

Own compute.
Operate less.

01

Local by default.

Your steady-state workloads should run where your hardware and data already live.

02

Cloud when useful.

External capacity should be an overflow valve, not the only place your application can exist.

03

Explain the machine.

Developers should understand why a workload was placed, blocked, slowed down or moved.

04

Measure the overhead.

Every Nodeau layer should justify its latency. The hot path stays intentionally thin.
