01

Model-aware fleet placement

Choose a worker using VRAM headroom, runtime compatibility, model locality, queue depth, thermals and health — not simply whether a GPU exists.

02

Policy-driven cloud overflow

Keep steady workloads on owned hardware, then spill to external capacity when queue delay, availability or a spending policy says to.

03

Team access & workload policy roadmap

Role-aware access, project boundaries, quotas and workload priorities for teams that need more than a shared shell account.

04

Audit & deployment history roadmap

Know who changed a deployment, which model/runtime revision ran, where it was placed and what configuration can be rolled back.

05

Health, utilization & performance

See queue time, TTFT, tokens per second, VRAM, temperature and power together so the team understands both AI performance and the hardware underneath it.

06

Explainable admission

Reject a deployment before it OOMs and explain the reason: not enough VRAM, context too large, incompatible runtime, unhealthy node, or policy limit.
